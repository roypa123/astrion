Astrion

Privacy policy

Applies to the Astrion mobile app for Android. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Astrion lets you explore the universe in 3D, follow rocket launches and sky events, and save your favorite objects. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We keep it to what the app needs.

Information you give us

To use Astrion you create an account. We store:

  • Your display name and email address. Used to run your account and sign you in.
  • Your password. We never store it as plain text. It is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • Your favorites. The objects you save, so they appear on every device you sign in on.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few things only on your phone. They are not sent to us:

  • Your sign-in token, in the phone's secure storage, so you stay signed in.
  • Whether you have seen the introduction, your recent searches, how many objects you have opened, and your chosen graphics quality.

Removing the app, or clearing its data, deletes all of this.

Ads

The app shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device and app information, and may show ads based on your interests. We do not receive this information. Google handles it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

What we do not collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • Payment information. Astrion has no purchases.

How information is used and shared

We use your information only to provide the app: signing you in, saving your favorites and showing content. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with other companies, apart from Google AdMob as described above.

The app talks to our servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Launch and sky-event information shown in the app comes from public sources and does not involve your personal data.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account information until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself at any time in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. Deleting removes your account, display name, email address, password and favorites. See how to delete your account.

Children

Astrion is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
