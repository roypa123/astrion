Astrion

Delete your account

You can delete your Astrion account and the data that goes with it at any time. It takes a few taps and cannot be undone.

In the Astrion app

  1. Open Astrion and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

What is deleted

  • Your account: display name, email address and password.
  • Your saved favorites.
Nothing else is kept about you. Page views are recorded anonymously with a random visitor ID that is not linked to your account, so there is nothing more to remove. After deleting, you can create a new account at any time.
